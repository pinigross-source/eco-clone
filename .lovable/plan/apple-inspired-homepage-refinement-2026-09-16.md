# Apple-inspired homepage refinement

## Direction
Transform the homepage into a bright, cinematic product-gallery experience. Use gallery white, soft silver, precise coral accents, generous whitespace, the current EnviroBiotics fonts, and the current dark green-charcoal text color. Preserve all existing copy, imagery, product facts, links, tracking, navigation, metadata, and section order.

## Layout improvements
- Refine the navigation into a slimmer translucent bar with clearer spacing, consistent icon targets, and stable mobile alignment.
- Recompose the opening section so desktop reads as one cinematic image-led moment and mobile avoids the current long stacked feeling while keeping the same image and wording.
- Tighten the trusted-venues area into a more deliberate transition between the opening story and product selection, with controlled logo sizing and less empty space.
- Polish the product selector as a premium gallery: stronger image scale, disciplined card proportions, clearer pricing and specifications, and responsive action placement.
- Give the science, wellness, and testimonial areas a consistent full-width rhythm using alternating gallery-white and soft-silver bands rather than disconnected floating sections.
- Reduce oversized gaps while preserving generous whitespace at intentional chapter breaks.

## Responsive and Apple-device quality
- Build mobile-first for 375–430px widths, then tune tablet, laptop, and wide desktop layouts.
- Use safe-area-aware spacing for iPhone, 44px minimum touch targets, readable 16px body copy, and stable image aspect ratios.
- Prevent horizontal overflow, clipped text, layout shifts, and accidental overlaps across portrait and landscape sizes.
- Keep motion subtle and purposeful, with reduced-motion support and no heavy effects that slow the page.
- Preserve optimized AVIF loading, eager-load only the opening image, and lazy-load below-fold media.

## Verification
- Check the complete homepage at iPhone and desktop sizes, including section boundaries and footer transition.
- Verify the menu, product scroll action, video dialog, product links, carousel controls, and Shopify handoffs.
- Confirm one H1, clean metadata, no browser errors, no horizontal overflow, and a successful build.

## Scope
- Homepage only. No content rewrites, new claims, product-data changes, backend changes, publishing, or unrelated route redesigns.
